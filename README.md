### Fabrício de Oliveira

Professor and researcher in Agrometeorology and Environmental Data Science at UTFPR (Federal University of Technology – Paraná), Santa Helena campus, and leader of the GAMBI-TEC research group.
I work on climate, evapotranspiration, crop modeling and emulation, soils and remote sensing, mostly with Python and Google Earth Engine.

#### Python packages

| | |
|---|---|
| [**agrometeorologiapy**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/agrometeorologiapy) [![PyPI](https://img.shields.io/pypi/v/agrometeorologiapy?label=PyPI)](https://pypi.org/project/agrometeorologiapy/) | Agrometeorology library: solar radiation, temperature, humidity, energy balance, evapotranspiration, degree-days, water balance and the FAO Agro-Ecological Zone crop model. |
| [**cropemu**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/emulator_crop) [![PyPI](https://img.shields.io/pypi/v/cropemu?label=PyPI)](https://pypi.org/project/cropemu/) | Neural-network emulators of DSSAT CROPGRO-Soybean and FAO AquaCrop, trained on ~118,000 simulated seasons worldwide. They predict phenology, yield, biomass and water balance in under a millisecond, with NASA POWER weather and SoilGrids soil. Includes the [CropEmu web app](https://fcoliveira-utfpr.github.io/emulator_crop/) for in-season crop monitoring. |

#### Featured research

| | |
|---|---|
| [**CROPGRO_soybean_br**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/CROPGRO_soybean_br) | Nationwide maturity-group calibration of CROPGRO-Soybean (DSSAT 4.8) for Brazil, inversion of MODIS phenology into the effective maturity group and sowing date (2000–2024), and water limitation of yield. [Interactive atlas](https://fcoliveira-utfpr.github.io/CROPGRO_soybean_br/). |
| [**soybean_yield_forecasts**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/soybean_yield_forecasts) | Soybean yield forecasts for 49 municipalities of western Paraná (2001–2025) from MODIS, ERA5-Land and CHIRPS: season-blocked validation, spatio-temporal error structure and shortfall risk. |
| [**ML_maize_pr**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/ML_maize_pr) | Hybrid FAO Agro-Ecological Zone + machine learning model for second-season maize yield in the 399 municipalities of Paraná, with economic risk analysis of the sowing date. |
| [**eto_cfa_nasapower**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/eto_cfa_nasapower) | Daily reference evapotranspiration from NASA/POWER in Brazil's humid subtropical (Cfa) climate: Penman-Monteith, empirical equations and machine learning, validated at 90 INMET stations. |
| [**IDFTec**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/IDFTec) | Intensity-Duration-Frequency (IDF) curves for 5,569 Brazilian municipalities from annual maximum daily rainfall (1961–2025, Xavier BR-DWGD). |
| [**climate_paraguay**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/climate_paraguay) | [ClimaTec PY](https://fcoliveira-utfpr.github.io/climate_paraguay/): Köppen-Geiger, Thornthwaite and Holdridge life zones for Paraguay at ~1 km (1991–2020 normals), with an interactive web atlas. |
| [**change_climate**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/change_climate) | Is the climate close to changing class? Köppen and Thornthwaite with moving 30-year reference windows (1990–2025) for Brazilian state capitals, measuring the margin of each criterion to its threshold. |
| [**climate_for_soil**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/climate_for_soil) | CHELSA climate covariates for the MapBiomas Soil models: reproduction, comparison of climate classifications and homogeneous zones. |
| [**climate_parana**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/climate_parana) | Hydroclimatic and thermal characterization of agroclimatic zones in Paraná at the municipal scale. |
| [**climas_brasil**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/climas_brasil) | Köppen-Geiger, Thornthwaite and Camargo climate classifications for Brazil by administrative unit. |
| [**ETo_oeste_pr**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/ETo_oeste_pr) · [**nasapower_validation**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/nasapower_validation) · [**chuva_probabilidade_sh**](https://github.com/fcoliveira-utfpr/chuva_probabilidade_sh) | Alternative ETo methods with NASA/POWER data in western Paraná, accuracy of NASA/POWER for ETo in humid Brazil, and probabilistic rainfall modeling for Santa Helena ([RBGF, 2025](https://doi.org/10.26848/rbgf.v18.2.p1527-1542)). |
